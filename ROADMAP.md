## Open (found by the creative hub's HUB-02 and HUB-05 runs, 2026-10-22)

Seen on the master build (2a21970) while Claude translated *The Clockless God* VN script and *The Hour That Happens Twice* into Croatian through the client provider. Each item is its own ticket.

- **BTT-15: QA flags kept lines in VN folders as "untranslated".** `deterministicChecks` in `pipeline/qa.ts` marks a segment as a major "untranslated" when the target equals the source and has at least 3 words. In a VN folder the speaker tag counts as words, so a line kept on purpose (`<1>INÈS (angry):</1> Basszus.`, French and Latin lines, "VII de IX") becomes a major: 14 of 37 chunks in HUB-02 and 7 of 69 in HUB-05 were flagged falsely, each with a recheck and a fix round. Count only the text outside inline tags, and let the brief's "kept as is" rule (or a glossary row marked keep) silence it.
- **BTT-16: the glossary pass overwrites the brief.** `runner.ts:174-175` replaces a brief set with `set_brief` before `start_translation` with the brief the first glossary pass returns. The editorial rules of the user's brief are lost until `set_brief` is called again, and chunks leased in between run without them. Merge instead: keep the user's brief and add the glossary pass's fields that are empty.
- **BTT-17: glossary matching is a substring match.** Matching is case-insensitive substring with a cap of 60 entries per chunk, so short sources fire inside other words ("Da" in "day" and "Madame", "trap" in "trapped") and push real terms out; QA's glossary check also misses inflected target forms (Croatian cases), giving false minor issues. Match whole words, and for the QA check accept a target stem.
- **BTT-18: an EPUB export keeps the English cover.** The cover PNG carries the English title and tagline, and the alt text is English ("Cover of ..."), in *Bezsatni bog* and *Sat koji se dogodi dvaput*. Offer a translated cover (the title drawn on the source art, or a plain title page) and translate the alt text.
- **BTT-19: a stale `engine.lock` blocks the start when its pid is reused.** A lock from 2026-10-20 held pid 15408; on 2026-10-22 that pid was an unrelated `svchost.exe`, so the engine refused to start until the file was deleted by hand. Check that the pid is a Book Translator engine (process name, or a start time written into the lock) before refusing.
- **BTT-20: client task loop ergonomics.** `wait_for_progress` caps `timeoutSec` at 60 and returns as soon as any role has a task waiting, so a worker for one role cannot block on its own work (add a `role` filter); a task leased by a client that stopped holds the pipeline until its 30-minute lease runs out (about 25 minutes of stall in HUB-02; a shorter lease, a heartbeat, or a `release_client_task` for every lease of a dead session would help); minor QA findings never reach a fixer, so they stay in the text until an editor reads the QA list.
